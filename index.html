By Order of the Cat

The human has left. You, a cat of great ambition, must convince Bruno, a loyal and very literal golden retriever, that you are now in charge.

Single player · keyboard · progress saves automatically

How to play

  1. Explore the room. Walk with WASD or arrows, jump with Space. Glowing rings mark things you can use with E.
  2. Find leverage. Notes, a crown, Bruno's ball… each thing you find unlocks new lines to say.
  3. Talk to Bruno. Walk up and press E. Pick one line per conversation. Good lines raise his belief; rude ones backfire.
  4. Reach 100% belief before you run out of conversations and Bruno obeys your decree.
  5. Grow your kingdom. Earn Authority 👑 to buy Royal Court upgrades. Your treasury keeps earning while you're away.
